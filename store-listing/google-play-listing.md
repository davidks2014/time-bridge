## FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)

Some things are too important to leave unsaid.

Time Bridge lets you write letters, record messages, and leave memories for the people you love — to be delivered after you are gone.

**Leave the words that matter most**

Write a letter to your child. Record a voice message for your grandchildren. Share a memory your family never knew. With Time Bridge, you decide what you want to say, and to whom. Your messages are kept safe until the moment they are needed.

**How it works**

1. Create your message — write, upload a photo, or record a voice note.
2. Choose who receives it — your spouse, children, grandchildren, or close friends.
3. Set your delivery trigger — your message is sent only when the time is right.

It is that simple. No complicated setup. No technical knowledge needed.

**Proof of Life — your safety check-in**

Time Bridge checks in with you regularly. As long as you respond, your messages stay safely with us. If we are unable to reach you, your appointed Guardians are notified before anything is sent. You are always in control.

**Guardian system — people you trust**

You choose your own Guardians — family members or close friends you trust. They are the ones who confirm when delivery should happen. Nothing is sent without their involvement. Your privacy and your wishes are always respected.

**Your privacy is protected**

Time Bridge is built for Singapore. Your data is stored securely in Singapore and is never shared with third parties. All messages are encrypted. Only you — and the people you choose — can ever access your words.

We take the trust you place in us seriously. Your memories are precious. We treat them that way.

**For Singaporeans who care about family**

Many of us carry things we have never said — gratitude, love, advice, apologies. Time Bridge gives you the space and the time to say them properly, with care and thought, so your loved ones can hold on to your words long after you are gone.

Start with one message today. Your family will be grateful you did.

**Download Time Bridge — because love should not be left unspoken.**

*Time Bridge is available for free. Data is stored securely in Singapore. We respect your privacy and will never sell your information.*
